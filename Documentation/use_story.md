When the website loads the user will be greeted with welcome to our project. After reading the title underneath is a short summary of our project which gives the user the chance of exploring new genres, music. They can start right away with their discovery through our guessing game which has 3 difficulties easy, medium, and hard each increases the max amount of random genres to guess from. The instructions on how to play the game are placed a bit futher down the page. When the player chooses a difficulty the page now shows a giant blurred track cover with choices of genre at the bottom. At the top of the page the player can see how many rounds have passed and time left on the current round. When they select their guess and click the blurred image becomes green if its correct, red if its wrong. The track and genre is also named underneath the image for those that are curious or liked what they heard. At the end of the game a generic game complete page is shown with a score, amount correct and buttons to play again, view the scoreboard and go back home. If the user doesn't want to play the game to discover new music they can just go straight to generating a playlist by clicking the button named generate playlist. The user will then see a search by dropbox with 4 options genre, album/song and mood. Giving the repective general choice on the right, the website will try and generate song choices that match when clicking the giant generate playlist button

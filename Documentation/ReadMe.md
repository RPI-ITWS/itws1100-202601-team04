**Target Audience**: Music enthusiasts who enjoy casual gaming and discovering new songs.

**User Goals**:
- Test their knowledge of music across genres.
- Have fun while learning about new songs and artists.
- Share their scores and compete with friends.

**Pain Points**:
- Difficulty recognizing songs from short clips.
- Limited accessibility for users with hearing impairments.
- Frustration with repetitive or overly obscure song choices.

**Game Features**:
- Genre selection to tailor the experience.
- Random snippets of music to keep the game fresh.
- Multiple-choice buttons for guessing song names.
- Social sharing options to compare scores with friends.

**Tone and Style**:
- Fun, engaging, and slightly competitive.
- Bright and modern design with intuitive controls.
